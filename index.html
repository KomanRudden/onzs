FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Website design is free, including 3 free changes. After that there is a monthly fee of R150 - R450 depending on website size. It covers hosting, your own domain, care and small updates. No contract, cancel anytime.

What does the monthly fee include?

Every plan includes hosting, SSL, backups, email support and your own domain set-up (you pay the yearly domain fee). Starter has 1 small update a month, Standard 2 and Full 4. Updates are things like text, photo and price changes.

Do I own my domain and content?

Yes. Your domain name and your content are yours.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can redesign it, move it to better hosting, or just take over looking after it.

Do you only work with businesses in George?

No. We're based in George, but we work with clients anywhere. Everything can be handled over email.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content is ready. We'll give you a timeline up front.
