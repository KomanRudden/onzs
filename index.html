FAQ

Common questions

What's included in the R450/month care plan?

Your website, hosting, SSL certificate, and ongoing small updates like text, photo and price changes. The exact inclusions are confirmed in your quote.

Do I own my domain and content?

Yes. Your domain name and your content are yours.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. I can redesign it, move it to better hosting, or just take over looking after it.

Do you only work with businesses in George?

No. I'm based in George, but I work with clients anywhere. Everything can be handled over email.

What kind of custom software do you build?

Web applications, internal tools, integrations between systems you already use, and automation of manual work. Email me a description and I'll tell you honestly whether it's a good fit.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content is ready. You'll get a timeline with your quote.
